- `SECOND_PASS_TEST_RESULTS.md`: deterministic test summary from the initial validation pass.
- `qilian_results_final.json`: 22-frame metadata-planning summary; no Qilian raster collection was downloaded.

Reviewer-revision evidence is stored separately in `revision/` and includes the maintained LiCSBAS step-01 per-path SHA-256 comparison plus a concise revision-validation summary. This separation preserves the historical initial-submission records while making the revised evidence explicit.

User-specific paths are redacted. These files do not establish global archive compatibility, transfer-speed superiority, scientific pixel validity, LiCSBAS step 03+ processing, inversion or geophysical correctness.
